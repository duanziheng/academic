- Journal years follow the published volume/issue year; preprints follow the original arXiv submission year. Conference names use CIKM 2025 / AAAI 2019.
- Authors and BibTeX follow DOI publisher metadata and the printed byline. Consortium membership expansions are excluded from the main author list. For consortium-only contributions, keep the consortium name and an explicit participation note.
- The PTSD and 388-brain bylines were checked against the published PDFs on 2026-09-11. No equal-contribution or corresponding-author marker is added without verified evidence.
- Preprint titles, author order, and PDF links follow the current arXiv record; historic local PDF URLs remain available.
- The two 2023/2024 invited-talk labels are supported by the September 2026 owner-provided CV. SCORCH 2026 remains Talk until its invitation/presentation category is confirmed. Keep old talk routes stable.
- The downloadable CV is the unmodified September 2026 cv_ziheng (4).pdf supplied by the owner. Replace dist/files/Ziheng-Duan-CV.pdf and the displayed update date together when a new CV is supplied. The homepage Yale title follows the owner’s explicit instruction; the CV records the UCI appointment through October 2026.
- Citation filenames include a paper identifier and year. Every publication has a citation; links use PDF, Code, Project/Data when verified, Cite, then DOI/arXiv.
- ACM PDF and UCI abstract endpoints returned 403 in the audit environment; their official endpoints are retained. The unavailable wudufan/deep_brain repository link was removed.
- Every Netlify build runs scripts/check-site.py. This verifies local resources, anchors, citation years, and the CV before deployment. External resources should be checked during content updates; a 403 does not by itself establish that a publisher link is broken.
